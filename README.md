Sunset Platinum is the offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5: all 55 official trophies with their names, descriptions and grades, a guide in our own words for each one, a five-stage roadmap to the platinum, the missable trophies flagged, and a checklist that saves your progress in the browser.

## What it does

- **Checklist** — all 55 trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze). Tick one off when you earn it; progress is saved locally and survives a reload.
- **Guide pane** — click any trophy for its full guide, its sources, a per-trophy note, and previous/next. Every guide is written in our own words from the official description and says plainly where a place or step is not pinned down.
- **Roadmap** — five stages in the order to play them: Story, Side characters, Activities & skills, Collectibles & exploration, Money & cleanup.
- **Missables** — the 16 trophies tied to story missions or chapters, flagged with why they can be missed. The official list does not say whether story missions can be replayed, so each is treated as a one-shot.
- **Next up** — the next three trophies to chase, in roadmap order.
- **Search & filters** — search by name, filter by kind (story / activities / collectibles) and grade, and show earned-only.
- **Spoiler setting** — hides the names and descriptions of story trophies you have not earned yet.
- **Export / import** — your progress and notes as JSON, so you can move them to another browser.
- **Fully offline** — static files only, no network requests, no fonts, no images, no Rockstar artwork.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

## Sources

Names, descriptions and grades: the official PlayStation 5 trophy list, as published on Exophase. PSN progress counts: PSNProfiles. The guides are our own words and are not yet checked in the game.
